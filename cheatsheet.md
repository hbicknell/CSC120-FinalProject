# Available Commands 

* Show Options --- Shows Player the Available Commands --- Serves function of help command. 
* Go Forward --- Player goes forward in the map layout
* Go Back || Go Backward --- Player goes backward in the map layout
* Describe --- gives player description of an item 
* Explore --- allows player to explore area and find the hidden items within it 
* Pick Up --- allows player to pick up item 
* Put down -- allows player to put down item
* Invesitgate --- ONLY IN LAST AREA: allows player to investigate the puzzle box

# Locked Door 
There are two ways to get through the locked door. 
If you pick strength at the beginning your score will be high enough to kick the door down.  
Or If you have the key in your inventory you will be prompted to try the key in the door and it will unlock. 
NOTE: If you have the key in your inventory it will take priority over asking if you would like to kick the door down. 

# Puzzle Box 
The puzzle box has three 'slots'/'places'/'divets' in it that requires three stone spheres you will find in the map. 
The color dots in front of each slot corresponds to a spheres color opposite on the color wheel. For example if a dot in front of a slot is green, that means the ruby sphere goes in that slot.
The Solution Is: 
First Slot: Ruby/Red Rock
Second Slot: Amethyst/Purple Rock
Third Slot: Lapis Lazuli/Blue Rock
